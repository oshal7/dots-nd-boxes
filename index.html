How to play

  • Players take turns drawing one line between two adjacent dots.
  • Complete the 4th side of a box to capture it (+1 point) and take a bonus turn.
  • Chain captures to rack up combos and sweep the board.
  • When every line is drawn, the most boxes wins. Equal scores tie.

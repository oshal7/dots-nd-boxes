# Critic 1: score3d (lab 0–5.5 = film 10.0–15.5)

**Verdict: REVISE.** The core idea reads well: at the 3.0 hold (both aspects) the height difference makes the winner obvious, the rounded cubes and the board base look premium, labels track their towers, and nothing sits still (no span with frame-diff YAVG < 0.15 lasting ≥ 0.2 s). The blue field measures **45,105,215** against the 47,107,216 target, which is within encode tolerance. White on that blue is **5.1:1**, and ink on paper/card is well above 4.5:1. Four defects keep it off the bar. Evidence is in `critic1/` (sheet-*, d0-*, d2-h, d3-*, h/crop_*, v/trio).

## Defects (most severe first)

1. **The "tower top rushes the camera" is a single cube breaking off (both aspects, 3.55–3.87, right third in H, centre in V).** One top cube detaches and tilts toward the lens while the camera stays locked. For about 0.3 s the tower shows a notch and a floating part. Mia's 10 becomes 9 on screen just after the score was shown. This breaks the no-floating-parts rule, and it reads as a thrown prop rather than a Contra-style fly-through. The largest frame-diff spike is 3.83 (24.7 H / 18.9 V). The handoff to flat blue is a cube face with a gradient that jumps to flat colour in about 0.1 s.
2. **The lighting is not one key plus a rim (H, whole 3D section).** In H 3.0 the red tower's shadow falls to the left and the blue tower's to the right. That is a central overhead point light, not a soft key from one side. The blue tower has **no contact shadow** at its base (`h/crop_blue.png`, bottom right). Its cast shadow starts well to the right of the cubes, so the tower looks like it floats above the paper.
3. **Muddy lighting dip at 0.07–0.27 (both aspects, the board).** The board goes grey and desaturated and has no base. A sample reads 146,176,230 → 130,158,207 → 147,174,225. Then at 0.30 the base and warm card appear in a single step (`h/cmp01.png`). This reads as a crossfade between two renders, not a lit object tilting into view.
4. **V framing (0.5–3.7).** The subject sits right of centre with the left 35% empty (V 2.0). From 1.5 to 3.7 the red tower touches or clips the left edge (x ≈ 0–10). At 3.0 the bottom ~420 px is empty paper, and "Mia Wins!" floats about 300 px above the scene. In H, the board plus towers fill only about 45% of the frame until 2.4.
5. **Minor.** During the final landings (H 2.2–2.55) the cubes arc out through the top of the frame and back in, and the camera jumps visibly between 2.47 and 2.50. The fast cube has no motion blur. On the blue card (4.45–5.05, YAVG ≈ 0.15–0.2) the only motion is the dot's ring, which is close to the 0.5 s stillness limit. The staggered "Play it / your way." slides in clipped by the frame edge (4.0–4.1).

## Top 3 fixes (in code)

1. **Replace the cube throw with a camera dolly.** Keep the tower intact. Animate the `camera.position` and target from the 45° view down onto Mia's top face with an ease-in (for example, expo-in over 0.35 s). Reduce FOV slightly as it goes, so the top face fills the frame through perspective. Tone the face material toward #2f6bd8 over the last 3–4 frames, then cut to the field. The pixels on that last frame will match, so there is no gradient jump.
2. **Relight.** Remove the central point light. Use one large soft `DirectionalLight` key from upper left with `shadow.radius` and `mapSize` of 2048 or more, plus a rim light behind on the right, and hemisphere fill. Add an AO or contact-shadow pass, or a small blurred dark decal under each tower footprint, so both towers sit on the paper.
3. **Fix the opening and the V layout.** Keep the flat-to-tilt transition in one render. Start the 3D board lit, with the base present at a camera tilt of 0°, and animate only the tilt, with no opacity crossfade. For V, set the look-at target to the centroid of board plus towers. Frame so the red tower keeps at least 60 px of margin, move the camera closer (or use a narrower FOV) so the subject fills about 70% of the height, and position "Mia Wins!" relative to Mia's projected tower top instead of at a fixed top offset.
